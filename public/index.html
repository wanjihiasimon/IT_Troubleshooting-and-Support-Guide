A REPEATABLE PROCESS

Good support starts with good questions.

Work from low risk to high impact. Record what changed and confirm the fix with the person who reported the issue.

01

Understand

Who is affected? What changed? When did it start? Capture the exact error message.

02

Scope

Check whether this affects one device, one person, one location, or everyone.

03

Test safely

Start with power, cables, settings, and built-in diagnostics. Change one thing at a time.

04

Verify & document

Retest the original task, record the result, and hand off with useful details if unresolved.

ESCALATION & HANDOFF

Know when to stop and get help.

Escalate early when the issue could affect safety, data, security, or many people. Follow your organization’s incident process and priority definitions.

URGENT

Contact IT or security now

  • Suspected phishing with credentials entered, malware, ransomware, or unexpected MFA requests.
  • Lost or stolen work device, suspected data exposure, or a damaged/swollen battery.
  • A widespread outage, critical business service unavailable, or possible data loss.
SUPPORT REQUEST

Share a useful incident summary

  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • Exact error text, date/time, device name, app, and recent changes.
  • Who and what are affected; what you already tried and the result.
  • Best way to reach you and whether there is a safe workaround.

Never include passwords, one-time codes, recovery keys, or sensitive files in a ticket.
